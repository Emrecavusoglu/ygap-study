# YGAP source and submission review

23 September 2026. The current claims are supported within the scope described below. Numerical tables were preserved. The package remains for co-author review, with several factual declarations and final journal checks outstanding.

## Temperament evidence

Dib et al. 2021. Relevant publisher Methods and Results support the four higher nonhyperthymic dimensions in patients and the opposite hyperthymic pattern. Participants with schizophrenia had been hospitalized for more than one year and used an Arabic TEMPS-A version. The manuscript now specifies the inpatient setting and keeps the partial-comparison interpretation. It does not compare raw scores across language versions.

[Dib et al. publisher full text](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13104-021-05854-8)

Fico et al. 2023. The PubMed abstract and author-institution full text identify 799 enrolled patients with bipolar disorder and 717 complete cases used for clustering, a short TEMPS-A version and the modified overt aggression scale. The clusters incorporated both measures. Their temperament differences therefore provide descriptive context, not independent confirmation of temperament predicting offending. The Discussion now states this explicitly. No regression coefficient or treatment effect is imported into our study.

[Fico et al. full article in the University of Barcelona repository](https://diposit.ub.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/f77f6885-3e76-4b87-801c-b34b0ee23107/content)

Çatlı et al. 2026. Publisher Methods and Table 3 confirm outpatient recruitment in 2016, with 63 judicially involved and 40 comparison patients. None of the five temperament comparisons was significant. Their definition concerns documented judicial involvement, not a legal finding of guilt. The contrasting result remains prominent; recruitment year was clarified. Differences in setting may matter, but do not explain the discrepancy conclusively.

[Çatlı et al. publisher article and PDF](https://psychiatry-psychopharmacology.com/index.php/pub/article/view/3566)

## Validation and contextual evidence

Vahip et al. 2005. The foundational Turkish psychometric source remains appropriate despite its age. Its abstract contains inconsistent 99/100-item descriptions. Our 99-item description is based on the administered form confirmed by the team, not an attempted reconstruction of that discrepancy. The publisher-indexed 2006 corrigendum corrects irritable-temperament values in Table 5; it does not establish a new scoring rule. No corrected normative value or dominant-temperament threshold is used in our analyses. Access level remains abstract plus indexed correction, not complete full-text verification.

[Vahip et al. PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15780682/)

[Vahip et al. publisher corrigendum record](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0165032706000425)

Whiting et al. 2022. Relevant publisher sections confirm the association between schizophrenia-spectrum disorders and violence perpetration. The synthesis concerns defined diagnostic and outcome groups, with substantial heterogeneity. It supports the bounded introductory statement, not a universal claim about psychiatric patients, all crime, sex ratios or individual dangerousness.

[Whiting et al. JAMA Psychiatry full text](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamapsychiatry/fullarticle/2787197)

Lagerberg et al. 2025. Publisher Methods and Results confirm a longitudinal risk-factor synthesis supporting criminal history and substance misuse as contextual risk markers. The study excluded selected solely-offender populations and found weaker estimates in inpatient analyses. It informs possible unmeasured differences; it does not supply effect estimates applicable to our forensic cohort or establish treatment causality.

[Lagerberg et al. publisher full text](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/the-british-journal-of-psychiatry/article/systematic-review-of-risk-factors-for-violence-in-psychosis-10year-update/4F06DDCCDDC218E7C8D3239B5315744F)

Scope: this is a check of the claims retained in the manuscript, not a systematic literature search, replication of external studies or exhaustive retraction screen. The six-reference list is retained; no reference was added merely to increase its length.
