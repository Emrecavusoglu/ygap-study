# Phase 3 — Cross-check the literature

Status: all 17 original references triaged individually; five proposed additions and the foundational validation source mapped to claims. Access depth varies and is stated explicitly.

## What was checked

The [source audit](../../references/SOURCE_AUDIT.md) separates bibliographic identity, the population/construct studied, manuscript claim compatibility, and reading limitations. Primary publisher pages and PubMed records were used. This is a targeted citation audit, not a systematic review and not a claim that every full text or supplementary analysis was obtained. Unrelated web-search results were discarded.

The key problem is claim relevance, not publication age. Retain the Turkish TEMPS-A validation even though it is old. Replace broad risk assertions with more recent synthesis, while keeping older local studies only where they provide specific historical context.

## Decisions supported by the audit

1. Remove the assertion that recent research finds no increase in criminality across psychiatric disorders. The cited Ayar review does not support that wording, and violence is not interchangeable with all crime.
2. Remove the universal male tenfold risk assertion unless an appropriate population, comparator and outcome can be documented. Homicidal-patient studies cannot supply a general-population risk ratio.
3. Keep affective temperament separate from character, legal cynicism, psychopathy, current depressive symptoms and clinical aggression.
4. Include both supporting and contrasting direct comparisons. Dib supports four patient–control score differences but reports a different hyperthymic pattern. Çatlı reports a null offender/non-offender temperament comparison.
5. Use Fico only as bipolar-disorder aggression context. Use Whiting and Lagerberg for appropriately bounded epidemiological context, not proof that TEMPS-A predicts crime.
6. Verify the Vahip corrigendum and administered instrument. A corrigendum was located but its substantive correction was not retrieved; do not infer what it changed.
