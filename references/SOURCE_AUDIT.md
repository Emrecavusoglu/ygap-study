# Source-by-source citation audit

Audit date: 19 September 2026. Scope: all 17 references in the supplied manuscript, plus five priority additions. Vahip is both an original reference and a foundational source to retain. This is a claim-support audit, not a systematic review or independent replication of the cited studies.

Reading levels are deliberately conservative: **full-text sections** means relevant methods/results/discussion were accessible; **abstract** means conclusions are limited to the abstract; **metadata/partial** means bibliographic identification is available but detailed findings are not certified. Merely locating a URL does not establish that its full text was read. Suggested removal refers to the current manuscript claim, not to the quality of the cited work.

## Original references

### 1. Gkotsi and Gasser, 2016

[Publisher record](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0160252716300437). Int J Law Psychiatry 46:58–67. DOI 10.1016/j.ijlp.2016.02.030. **Level: metadata/abstract scope; full-text verification incomplete.** The article concerns ethical/legal implications of neuroscience in dangerousness assessment. That scope does not establish a recent population-level disappearance of violence or offending risk. Remove it from that epidemiological sentence; retain only if the paper actually discusses assessment ethics.

### 2. Ayar and Ayar, 2021

[Primary journal page and abstract](https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/pub/sabited/article/1074174). Abant Sağlık Bilimleri ve Teknolojileri Dergisi 1(2):83–91. **Level: abstract.** The review describes evidence of increased crime/violence and variation by disorders and additional risk factors. It does not support the manuscript's attribution that recent research shows no difference from the public. Replace the broken URL with the journal record. Use a recent systematic synthesis for the rewritten epidemiological paragraph.

### 3. Bailey and MacCulloch, 1992

[Publisher record](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/09585189208409021). The Journal of Forensic Psychiatry 3:445–461; DOI 10.1080/09585189208409021. **Level: metadata/partial abstract; full text outstanding.** The population is patients discharged from a special hospital and the outcome is reconviction/aftercare. Do not extrapolate this to all psychiatric patients or a universal male-to-female crime ratio. Retain only for a specific post-discharge context after checking methods and follow-up.

### 4. Hwang and Segal, 1996

[PubMed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8929663/). Int J Law Psychiatry 19(1):93–105; DOI 10.1016/0160-2527(95)00029-1. **Level: abstract and metadata.** Correct Hwang, not Hawing. Sheltered-care residents had lower arrest rates for most crime categories but higher violent-crime arrest rates than the California comparator; prior offending, age, substances and male sex were reported predictors. Arrests, violence and total criminality are distinct outcomes. This is contextual historical evidence, not a current universal estimate. Full text was blocked by the PMC access check.

### 5. Türkcan et al., 2000

[Primary journal record](https://dusunenadamdergisi.org/article/886). Düşünen Adam 13(3):132–137. **Level: primary metadata; detailed result re-extraction outstanding.** Correct the in-text 1997/2000 mismatch. The journal lists Türkcan, İncesu, Canbek, Can, Sercan and Uygur; the supplied bibliography omitted Uygur. A series of 1,831 forensic cases can describe referred cases and diagnosis/offense patterns, but cannot alone estimate population incidence or causation. Do not insert unverified percentages from this paper.

### 6. Ong et al., 2009

[Publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/00048670903001976). Aust N Z J Psychiatry 43(8):775–780. Title: Community outcomes of mentally disordered homicide offenders in Victoria. Authors: Ong, Carroll, Reid, Deacon. **Level: publisher metadata/abstract scope; full text outstanding.** Correct the corrupted title. A selected homicide-offender follow-up cannot establish the manuscript's assertion that men generally commit crimes ten times more often. Remove that use; retain only for an appropriately described community-outcome claim.

### 7. İnan, Yıldızhan and Öncü, 2018

[PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30887476/). Turk Psikiyatri Derg 29(4):258–268. **Level: bibliographic record and abstract access; detailed methods not certified.** Correct the supplied 1–10 page range. The study concerns homicidal forensic psychiatric patients; it should not supply a population-wide sex risk ratio. It may support a narrowly stated description of Turkish forensic homicide samples after extraction of the relevant result.

### 8. Martínez-López et al., 2019

[Publisher metadata](https://www.mdpi.com/1660-4601/16/23/4761/notes); [article](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16234761). Int J Environ Res Public Health 16(23):4761. **Level: metadata and study scope; full methods verification outstanding.** The publisher author list additionally includes Monroy and Fresán, missing from the supplied list. Psychopathic subtypes and temperament/character constructs must not be treated as equivalent to TEMPS-A dimensions in patients with psychotic disorders. Before retaining, verify the instrument and specific result in the full text. It cannot establish a causal pathway for the present study.

### 9. Ameri et al., 2019

[PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31122632/); [publisher](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijlp.2019.04.008). Int J Law Psychiatry 64:211–218. Authors: Ameri T, Burgason KA, DeLisi M, Heirigs MH, Hochstetler A, Vaughn MG. **Level: metadata/partial abstract; detailed findings outstanding.** Remove duplicated author initials/names in the bibliography. Legal cynicism is not a synonym for narcissistic personality disorder. The manuscript's narcissism/arrest-count interpretation needs the actual measures and model verified. Remove the paragraph provisionally; do not claim the paper never examined narcissism without the full text.

### 10. Aydoğdu and Saka, 2024

Chapter on dangerousness, security measures and compulsory admission, in Dinçmen Adli Psikiyatri, pp.130–138. [Supplied DOI](https://doi.org/10.5152/8214). **Level: chapter citation located; publisher PDF and DOI resolution incomplete.** The earlier audit incorrectly treated DOI length as proof of incompleteness. That assertion is withdrawn; 10.5152/8214 appears in the chapter citation. Confirm against the publisher/book before final Zotero entry. A discussion of law/assessment is not a source for this study's admission/discharge procedures unless those claims are specifically supported. Current jurisdiction-specific legal assertions require separate verification or narrower wording.

### 11. Vahip et al., 2005

[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15780682/). J Affect Disord 85(1–2):113–125; DOI 10.1016/j.jad.2003.10.011. Authors: Vahip S, Kesebir S, Alkan M, Yazici O, Akiskal KK, Akiskal HS. **Level: PubMed metadata/abstract; instrument/corrigendum full-text check outstanding.** Retain as the foundational Turkish psychometric source. A [2006 corrigendum record](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0165032706000425) was located, but its correction was not accessible. Do not infer its subject or certify the item count from conflicting descriptions. Confirm the actual questionnaire used and scoring key. Validation in clinically well participants does not establish violence prediction or validate every forensic application.

### 12. Kesebir et al., 2005

Supplied citation: Kesebir S, Vahip S, Akdeniz F, Yüncü Z. Bipolar Bozuklukta Mizaç ile Klinik Özelliklerin İlişkisi. Türk Psikiyatri Dergisi 16(3):164–169. **Level: unresolved exact record/full-text verification.** A PubMed author/year search returned a [candidate record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16180149/), but the readable record was not obtained; do not label it verified. Do not confuse this paper with the separate J Affect Disord 85:153–158 TEMPS-A bipolar/family study. The supplied title concerns bipolar clinical features, not a general demonstration of irritability in psychosis. Obtain the exact paper if retaining the switching claim.

### 13. Cassano et al., 1989

[PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2602525/). Psychopathology 22:278–288. **Level: metadata/title; full text outstanding.** Correct Cassano spelling. The title explicitly concerns primary major depression and redefining bipolarity using a semistructured interview. It should not be cited as direct evidence for higher irritable TEMPS-A scores across psychotic disorders. Remove this use; retain only if a historical affective-temperament argument is actually needed.

### 14. Tomaz, Moreira and Souza Cruz, 2023

[Publisher full text](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychiatry/articles/10.3389/fpsyt.2023.935755/full). Front Psychiatry 14:935755; DOI 10.3389/fpsyt.2023.935755. **Level: full-text scope/sections.** This systematic review concerns treatment/punishment of drug-using offenders and drug use/recidivism outcomes. It does not directly establish diminished self-control in all patients with psychosis or temperament causing homicide. Correct the malformed volume/date string; remove it from the psychosis self-control sentence.

### 15. Pompili et al., 2017

[Publisher full text](https://www.rivistadipsichiatria.it/archivio/2801/articoli/28344/). Riv Psichiatr 52(5):175–179; DOI 10.1708/2801.28344. **Level: relevant full-text sections.** A selective narrative review addresses heterogeneous aggression mechanisms and treatment. It provides background on aggression; it does not validate the present causal interpretation or equate irritable temperament with aggressive acts. Correct Nicolò spelling and normal journal date formatting. If retained, narrow the claim to heterogeneous clinical correlates.

### 16. Reynolds and McGowan, 2021

[PMC record](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8059489/). Int J Neuropsychopharmacol 24(4):253–255. **Level: bibliographic/title scope; full text access incomplete.** The article concerns depressive symptoms and antipsychotic treatment. Symptoms are not depressive temperament. Our data contain neither medication exposure nor temporal symptom change, and the supposed higher forensic depressive score is reversed. Remove the medication-causation explanation regardless of whether the paper discusses particular drug effects. Full-text review is needed before retaining a narrower treatment statement.

### 17. Placidi et al., 1998

[PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9476738/). J Affect Disord 47(1–3):1–10; DOI 10.1016/S0165-0327(97)00122-5. **Level: bibliographic/title scope; detailed full-text findings outstanding.** This is a TEMPS-I reliability/psychometric study in 1,010 students aged 14–26. TEMPS-I is not the self-report TEMPS-A; a student psychometric study does not establish that hyperthymia is specific to bipolar disorder. Remove the unsupported diagnostic-specificity use. A nonsignificant result in our groups cannot be explained simply by presumed bipolar proportions.

## Priority additions and exact uses

### A. Dib et al., 2021 — direct patient/control comparison

[Publisher full text](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13104-021-05854-8), [PMID 34823586](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34823586/). **Level: methods/results/discussion sections.** Cross-sectional comparison of 250 patients with schizophrenia and 250 controls in Lebanon. Four nonhyperthymic scores were higher in patients; hyperthymic scores were higher in controls. Symptom severity was also assessed. Use for a partly consistent patient/control pattern, explicitly noting the hyperthymic contrast. Its Arabic instrument and recruitment differ from ours; do not compare raw score magnitudes as if versions were identical or infer an offending association.

### B. Fico et al., 2023 — adjacent bipolar/aggression evidence

[PMID 36470551](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36470551/). J Affect Disord 323:327–335; DOI 10.1016/j.jad.2022.11.084. **Level: PubMed metadata/abstract; detailed model/full-text appraisal outstanding.** The report examines affective temperament and aggressive dimensions in 799 participants with bipolar disorder, using aggression measures rather than our forensic/inpatient grouping. Appropriate as adjacent background, not direct replication. Statistical mediation wording in a title does not establish temporal causation. Do not insert model coefficients or claim independent prediction without reading the full model.

### C. Whiting et al., 2022 — bounded violence epidemiology

[PMID 34935869](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34935869/). JAMA Psychiatry 79:120–132; DOI 10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2021.3721. **Level: metadata/abstract evidence.** The review concerns schizophrenia-spectrum disorders and violence perpetration, not all psychiatric illness and all crime. Use it to frame an association that varies across relevant risk contexts. Sex-specific relative odds compare patients with their corresponding population comparators; they must not be converted into a male-versus-female tenfold claim. Does not establish individual dangerousness or TEMPS-A prediction.

### D. Lagerberg et al., 2025 — longitudinal risk-factor context

[PMID 40091674](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40091674/). Br J Psychiatry 226:100–107; DOI 10.1192/bjp.2024.120. **Level: metadata/abstract evidence; detailed bias assessment outstanding.** Longitudinal synthesis identifies criminal history and substance misuse as relevant risk markers and treatment-related protective associations. Use to motivate contextual confounders missing from our dataset. Do not equate publication year with the latest included study date, claim treatment causality, or infer that temperament was an independently established risk factor.

### E. Çatlı et al., 2026 — closest contrasting Turkish study

[Publisher full text](https://www.psychiatry-psychopharmacology.com/index.php/pub/article/download/3566/2255). Psychiatry Clin Psychopharmacol 36(3):301–311; DOI 10.5152/pcp.2026.251292. **Level: methods/results sections; PubMed indexing not confirmed.** Single-center cross-sectional sample of 103 patients with schizophrenia: 63 with and 40 without documented offense-related judicial involvement. No between-group temperament differences were reported. Recruitment was through forensic/general outpatient clinics in 2016, unlike our forensic inpatient comparison. PANSS and insight measures were collected. Include the null finding and setting differences; do not reinterpret it as supporting our irritable group difference. Publication in 2026 does not mean data were collected in 2026.

## Boundaries and completion criteria

No inaccessible paper is certified fully checked. No exhaustive retraction/correction search across all 22 distinct works was completed; the identified Vahip correction is a specific open item. Full author metadata should be imported from verified records rather than manually reconstructed for the final bibliography. Older studies should be retained for relevant foundational/local contributions, not rejected on age alone. Obtain missing full texts only for claims retained in the final manuscript.
