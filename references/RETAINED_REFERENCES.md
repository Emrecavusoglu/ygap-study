## Instrument-form check

Its scoring notes contain cutoffs; the study nevertheless analyzes continuous scores. Scribd hosts a third-party copy and is an audit pointer, not a replacement for Vahip in the manuscript bibliography. No participant responses were accessed or reproduced.
