## Changes to the manuscript argument

Use the control group to contextualize broad patient–control differences, then discuss the forensic–outpatient contrast separately. Acknowledge the closest null finding and differences in setting. Do not assert a unique criminal temperament, diagnostic marker, independent causal factor, or clinical prediction performance. No first-ever claim is justified.
