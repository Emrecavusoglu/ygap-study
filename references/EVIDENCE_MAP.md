## Original reference triage

- Gkotsi 2016: legal/ethical neuroscience paper; do not use alone for population violence-rate estimates.
- Ayar 2021 and older risk-factor papers: retain only for claims they actually support; use current synthesis for general risk context.
- Martínez-López 2019: temperament/character in psychopathic subtypes is not equivalent to TEMPS-A in schizophrenia.
- Ameri 2019: legal cynicism must not be equated with narcissistic personality; recheck original claim or remove passage.
- Kesebir 2005 and Cassano 1989: bipolar/depression literature does not directly support a broad schizophrenia claim.
- Tomaz 2023: treatment/punishment in drug-using offenders does not directly establish impaired self-control in psychosis.
- Reynolds/McGowan 2021: depressive symptoms and treatment are distinct from depressive temperament. Remove unsupported causal application.
- Placidi 1998: TEMPS-I psychometrics should not be used alone to establish diagnostic specificity of hyperthymia.
- Correct Türkcan year mismatch, Hwang/Hawing and Cassano spelling, reference 9 author duplication, broken link in reference 2 and incomplete reference 10 DOI after checking originals.

## Search contribution template

For each additional paper record title, DOI/PMID, population, design, measure of temperament, definition of aggression/offending, result, limitation, exact manuscript claim supported and full-text screening status. Include null and contradictory results. Stop expanding when the main claims and closest comparisons are adequately covered.
