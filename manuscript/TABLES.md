## Table 3. TEMPS-A subscale scores and group comparisons

Forensic inpatients n = 66; outpatients n = 25; healthy controls n = 24. Subscale scores are presented as mean ± SD.

Both patient groups score higher than controls in depressive, cyclothymic, irritable and anxious dimensions (each Tukey p<.001). No hyperthymic pairwise contrast is significant. Tukey adjustment covers the three group comparisons within each dimension; there is no adjustment across all five dimensions.
